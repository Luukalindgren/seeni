# Model v2 report: more `muu` images of common unlisted fungi

Model: EfficientNet-B0 at 224 px, run `b0-224-muu2` (30 epochs, lr 3e-4), the same 44 classes as v1 (`docs/reports/phase7.md`). The only change is the `muu` training data. Produced with `seeni.evaluate`; T and the threshold were fixed on val with the phase 7 rule, then test was run once.

## Why

Two clear Google image screenshots, *Amanita fulva* and *Lactarius scrobiculatus*, were not recognized. Neither is a model class, so "en tunnista" was the right answer, but v1 gave *A. fulva* 61 % *Kuehneromyces mutabilis* and only the threshold stopped it. v1's `muu` had 5 998 images from 5 602 species, about one image per species, so the fungi people photograph most were barely in it. In the GBIF and iNaturalist Finnish observations only 22–23 of the 50 most photographed agaric-type species are model classes.

## Change

`seeni.muu index` now takes up to 40 observations of each of the 200 fungi with the most observations in the muu archive (FI, SE, NO, EE), then one each round-robin from the rest: 12 000 fungi images from 4 200 species (was 4 000 from ~4 000), plus 2 000 non-mushrooms as before. After dedup `muu` has 13 998 images. A rerun of a larger model (fine-tuned DINOv2 ViT-S/14, run `vits14-224-muu`) was tried first and rejected: species top-1 +1.3 points on val, but more dangerous confusions (46 vs 41 val images), about 43 MB instead of 8 MB, about 6× slower on the CPU, and it called the *A. fulva* screenshot *K. mutabilis* at 83 %.

## Results (test)

- **Fixed for `calibration.json`:** T = 0.7657, unknown threshold **0.67** (v1: 0.733 and 0.76).
- **Standard metrics:** top-1 86.1 %, top-3 95.8 %, macro-F1 0.826; species classes only top-1 85.1 %, top-3 95.1 % (v1 87.2 % and 95.9 % on the same species images: more listed-species images now go to `muu`). ECE 7.1 % → 2.9 % with T.
- **Dangerous confusion, in-model taxa:** raw 57 of 1 002 images, 16 after the threshold, **2 (0.2 %) after the threshold and lookalike warnings** (v1: 6). The two are *Galerina marginata* → `leccinum-versipelle-agg` and *Lactarius rufus* → `leccinum-versipelle-agg`, one image each.
- **Dangerous species not in the model (71 images):** 11 raw, 7 after the threshold, **0 after warnings**.
- **Unknown:** unlisted fungi (1 208 test images, now many from common species) get "en tunnista" 93.6 % of the time and a food mushroom among the candidates 5.6 % (v1 on its own test set: 90.2 % and 8.1 %). Non-mushrooms 100 %. Listed species are answered 81.9 % of the time, and then top-1 is right 95.8 %.
- **The two screenshots:** both are now `muu` on top (83 % and 94 %), so "en tunnista".

On the new val set, v1 and v2 compared directly: at the thresholds the rule picks for each, v2 answers 82.2 % of species images (v1 78.1 %) with the same rate of unlisted fungi answered with a food mushroom (7.3 % vs 7.4 %).

## Follow-ups

- Add the most photographed unlisted species as classes, starting with *Amanita rubescens*, *A. fulva*, *A. citrina*, *Coprinus comatus* and *Imleria badia*; their groups and lookalikes need expert review (#3, #9).
- *Lactarius turpis* (26th most photographed in Finland) has only one `muu` image; check its taxon key in the muu archive.
